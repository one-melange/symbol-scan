- [x] (T23) Incremental cache: coalesced + proportional writes — T4's per-save incremental (`Indexer.reindexFiles`) re-serialized and atomically rewrote the **whole** `IndexCache` (O(total symbols)) on every debounced FSEvents batch — a multi-MB write per save on a large repo, even for a one-file change.
